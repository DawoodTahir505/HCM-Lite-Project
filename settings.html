Company rules

How the rules work

  • Late: check-in after start time + grace minutes
  • Half day: worked under 5 hours
  • Absent: no record on a working day
  • Camera check: face recognized + inside the office radius
  • Leave counts working days only

Danger zone

Deletes the company, employees, attendance and leaves from this browser.
